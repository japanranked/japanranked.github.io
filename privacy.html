Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy covers "ranking-shorts", a small tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel Japan, Ranked to upload the channel's own videos.

What the tool does

The tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and their titles and descriptions to the owner's own YouTube channel, and to read basic information about that channel to confirm it is the right one. It is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to other people.

Information it uses

  • An access permission (OAuth token) that the channel owner grants with their own Google account. It is kept as an encrypted secret and used only to upload the owner's videos.
  • The videos, titles and descriptions the owner publishes.

The tool does not collect, store or share personal information about viewers or anyone else. It does not use cookies or analytics, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the channel's About page.
